# CapyFlow 0.4.11

- Join Global Chat from your profile menu.
- Turn message and Global Chat banners on or off in Settings → Notifications.
- Swipe up to dismiss a banner, or let it disappear after four seconds.
- Messages are grouped by date, so conversations are easier to follow.
- Starting a new chat is more reliable.
- Your profile picture fits properly at the top of Messages, including after returning from a chat.
- Listen to music while chatting, with the message box kept within reach.
- Find Messages in the navigation bar.
- See what someone is listening to when you open their profile.
- Add or remove songs in shared playlists with your collaborators.

Your account, playlists, messages and downloads stay with you when you update. Install the update over your existing CapyFlow app.
